# 3 starter prompts

Paste your details block (from `rate_rise.py`) where it says `[DETAILS BLOCK]`, replace the other `[BRACKETS]`, and use the AI assistant you already have. Check every number and date, then send it yourself. Use first names only; never paste bank details or addresses.

## 1. The classic price-rise letter

Write a short letter from me to a long-standing student announcing a rate change.

[DETAILS BLOCK]

Requirements:
- Open with something genuine about teaching them: [one real thing you value about working with this student].
- State the new rate and the start date in one plain sentence, no apology words such as "unfortunately", "sorry" or "I hate to".
- Give one honest reason in one sentence: [e.g. I have not changed my rate in four years and my costs have gone up].
- Say that their current lessons continue as normal until the start date.
- Invite questions, but do not ask for permission.
- End warmly, with my first name.
- Maximum 170 words. Plain language. No exclamation marks.

Give me two versions: one a little warmer, one a little more direct.

## 2. Short email version

Write a short email (subject line plus body, under 90 words) telling a student my lesson rate changes.
